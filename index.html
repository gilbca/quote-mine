Privacy Policy

Last updated November 12, 2026

Quote Mine has no server of its own and collects nothing. Your quotes are kept on your iPhone and in your own iCloud, where the developer can’t see them.

What Quote Mine keeps, and where

  • What you put in it: quotes, the names of the people who said them, dates, places, context, your collections and favorites, and your name if you give it. All of it is stored on your iPhone.
  • If you’re signed in to iCloud, it’s also kept in your private iCloud storage, so it comes back on a new iPhone. That storage belongs to your Apple Account and is covered by Apple’s privacy policy. The developer has no access to it.
  • If iCloud is off, it’s kept on your iPhone alone.

What Quote Mine doesn’t do

  • It has no accounts and no sign-in.
  • It has no analytics, no ads, no tracking, and no code from other companies that collects data.
  • It sells and shares nothing, because it has nothing to sell or share.

Location and places

  • Location is optional. Quote Mine asks for it only when you tap the location arrow while filling in where a quote was said, to suggest places near you.
  • Your location, and what you type in that field, go to Apple Maps to find places. Quote Mine doesn’t save your location. It saves only the name of the place you pick or type, with the quote.
  • You can turn location off for Quote Mine at any time in your iPhone’s Settings.

What you send

  • A quote card or a spreadsheet you send from Quote Mine goes only where you send it.
  • Words you share into Quote Mine from Messages or another app are saved as a quote, and nothing else is read.

What Apple may pass along

  • If you’ve chosen on your iPhone to share analytics with app developers, Apple gives the developer crash reports and usage numbers that don’t identify you.
  • If you test a beta through TestFlight, Apple shares crash reports and any feedback you send, and your name and email address if you were invited by email.
  • If you email the developer, your address and what you write are used only to answer you.

Deleting your data

  • Deleting a quote, a person, or a collection in the app removes it from your iPhone and from your iCloud.
  • Deleting the app removes what’s on your iPhone. What’s in iCloud can be removed from your iPhone’s iCloud storage settings.
  • The developer holds none of your data, so there’s nothing to ask him to delete. An email you sent is deleted if you ask.

Children

Quote Mine collects no data from anyone, children included.

Changes

Where are my quotes kept?

On your iPhone, and in your own iCloud if you’re signed in. With iCloud on, a new iPhone signed in to the same Apple Account gets them back by itself. Give it a minute the first time.

How do I save a quote from Messages?

  1. Touch and hold the message, then tap Select.
  2. Drag to select just the words they said.
  3. In the menu that appears, tap the arrow at its end, then Share.
  4. Tap Quote Mine.
  5. Pick who said it, then tap Add. You’re back in the chat.

The first time, Quote Mine may be under More, at the end of the row of apps. The steps are in the app too, under Settings, Save from Messages.

Can I bring in quotes from a spreadsheet?

Yes. Save the spreadsheet as a CSV file with a column named Person and a column named Quote. Date, Context, Quoted By, and Said At are read too when they’re there. Then open Settings, Import a Spreadsheet. You see what will come in before anything does, and a quote never comes in twice.

How do I get my quotes out?

Settings, Export a Spreadsheet makes a CSV file of every quote, which opens in Numbers or Excel and reads straight back into Quote Mine.
